# 04 · Thread architect

Turn this topic into an X thread.

Structure:
1. **Hook** — a specific, checkable claim or a surprising number. No preamble.
2. **Stakes** — why this matters now, in one post.
3. **Evidence** — 2–4 posts, each with a concrete example or source.
4. **The turn** — the counterargument or the thing everyone gets wrong.
5. **What I actually did** — the experiment, the setup, the result.
6. **Takeaway** — one sentence someone could repeat.
7. **Close** — a question that a knowledgeable person would want to answer.

Rules:
- 6–10 posts. Every post must survive being screenshotted alone.
- No "🧵", no "let's dive in", no "here's what I learned".
- Each post ≤ 280 characters (English).
- Mark [SOURCE?] wherever a claim needs a link.
- Give me the Chinese version as a separate, shorter thread (5–7 posts),
  rewritten for a Chinese audience — not translated.

Output as numbered posts, ready to paste.
