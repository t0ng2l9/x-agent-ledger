# 02 · Editor — single post (ChatGPT / Grok)

Act as an experienced technology journalist and editor.

Turn the following research into an original X post.

Requirements:

- Strong but not misleading opening.
- One clear central argument.
- Specific evidence or practical examples.
- Natural, concise English.
- Avoid generic AI marketing language.
- No unnecessary hashtags.
- Do not imitate or reproduce another creator's wording.

Provide:

1. English version.
2. Chinese version.
3. A longer thread outline.
4. A suggested visual concept.

Important: preserve factual accuracy and clearly identify opinions.

---

Then do this second pass before you hand it back:

- Cut the first sentence if the post still works without it.
- Replace every adjective that isn't doing measurable work.
- Check the character count of the English version (must be ≤ 280).
- Mark any claim that needs a link with [SOURCE?].
- Give me 3 alternative opening lines, ranked by how likely they are to stop
  a scroll — and say which one you'd pick and why.
